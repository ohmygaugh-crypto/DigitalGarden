[[GEN3]]
	Gen3 provides a data commons. Krumware provides a platfrom. This joint effort was to produce a template for insitutions to get started. A template they can wield without fear of vendor lock in. It's entirely opensrc. 
	Without this platform as a service many institutions simply won't be able to operate "Gen3". As it stands your average medical researcher typically doesn't have kubernetes expertise. Consequently, they can't participate with the data commons, Gen3 is, unless they can prop up the infrastructure themselves. Krum has a template you can follow to get up and running. *Note the canvas view is experimental and is NOT mobile friendly
