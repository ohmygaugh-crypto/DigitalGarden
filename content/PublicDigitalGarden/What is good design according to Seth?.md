# "The way" has already been spoken about. Many times over.
##### Every internet guru and design icon has some poetic blanket statement explaining some nuance of the human psych and how that interweaves with good design practice.

Maintaining *empathy* with the end user is the pinnacle mindset in "Design thinking". Stakeholders can gripe all they want, but this is what you ought fixate on. If they could've evoked change with their thinking they wouldn't have hired you in the first place.

This is to ensure you are building upon a foundation of shared experience between the people and the designers who may be out of touch to the realm of the product users.

# Be Bold

It's hard to not have imposter syndrome when you set-off, but you gotta trust your got and be bold with your decisions. Otherwise, save yourself time and get out of this industry. It's that frank. It's the only way to calibrate your mindset truthfully.

Believe you are setting the right expectations for others. If you are wrong then you are wrong. You fix the damage you caused. If you are indifferent then either things are going smoothly, or you are procrastinating on stating your opinion. Trust your gut. Be Decisive.

There's some Rick Rubin Quote I'm looking for in believing in ones abilities so much that you can get away with having no technical abilities. Oh here it is:

“I Have No Technical Ability” - Rick Rubin

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/rgOPHBLj6VA

I know that sounds like bullshit, but if you cycle-in some disciplined action items, the you can get the reps. If you get the reps then you can make well informed decisions about "the vibe" of the product. We are all human. We are all capable of creating something marvelous. To do so you can't compromise on your attention to detail. Thus, you gotta how to stay on top of something just as you find the balance to know when to step aside and let the more technical people navigate their craft.
