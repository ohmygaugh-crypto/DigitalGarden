## Platform Engineering is NOT:
- going to replace DevOps
- one size fit's most
- an assembly line for churning out the same self service developer environment.

## Platform Engineering IS: 
- nuanced to each organization
- an iterative process in need of timely evaluation.
- Ever "in flux"
