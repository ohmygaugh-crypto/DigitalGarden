Unoriginal, industry standard types of design thinking methodologies to consider on a case by case basis:
[Dot-voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dot-voting)
[Value-Stream mapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value-stream_mapping)
	[Kaizen Burst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaizen)
[Kano Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kano_model)
[Human-Centered Design](https://online.hbs.edu/blog/post/what-is-human-centered-design)
[Double Diamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_Diamond_(design_process_model))
[User Flow Mapping](https://www.radnolan.com/guides/user-flows)

These things are tried and true because any human can pick these up and apply them. Recognize that there are no imposters here. Only bad listeners.
